---

Compress your immediately preceding response into its essentials.

Rules:
- Summarize ONLY what you already said. Do not run tools, read
  files, search, or do new work.
- Lead with the single most important point.
- 3-6 bullets max, or one short paragraph if bullets don't fit.
- Keep concrete specifics — file:line refs, names, numbers, commands.
  Drop rationale, caveats, and alternatives you already ruled out.
- No preamble ("Here's a summary..."), no closing offer.
- If the previous response was already short, say so in one line
  instead of restating it.
